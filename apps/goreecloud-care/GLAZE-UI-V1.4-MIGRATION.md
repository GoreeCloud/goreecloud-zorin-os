## Current V1.4 contract

Care treats V1.4 as additive to the V1.3 token/component foundation and implements the current Optical Intelligence contract through a local deterministic resolver. The adapter covers:

- Content-Aware Frost bounds;
- Semantic Blur Protection;
- bounded depth hue shift;
- bounded environmental warmth;
- optional Environmental Color Memory with an 8% maximum influence;
- Forced Colors / HighContrast, Reduced Transparency, and Increased Contrast precedence;
- explicit local/no-telemetry/no-remote-context behavior.

Care does **not** collect wallpaper pixels, camera input, telemetry, analytics, network state, or remote context to drive optics. Default background complexity and daypart stay `unknown`; appearance comes from already-known local GTK presentation state. Any future environmental signal adapter requires separate privacy/security authority and must not be smuggled into Glaze presentation code.

## Native GTK mapping

GTK3 cannot claim compositor-authoritative backdrop sampling or the full web/native blur stack. Care therefore resolves the official numeric Optical Intelligence state and maps it to bounded native semantic classes. Unsupported optical effects degrade before content, focus, accessibility, or maintenance correctness.

Durable reading, findings, status, and consequential-action surfaces remain solid or near-solid. Optical treatment is restrained to application-owned presentation and never becomes operational authority.

Care also retains application-owned responsive desktop composition states:

- `compact`;
- `narrow-desktop`;
- `desktop`;
- `wide-desktop`.

Those states are Care layout behavior, not the definition of GLAZE UI V1.4 and not claims of Phone/Tablet/TV product support.
